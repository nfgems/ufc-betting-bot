Weigh-In Results | UFC 290: Volkanovski vs Rodriguez

The Fighters Step On The Scale In Las Vegas To Make It Official For The Peak Of International Fight Week 2023
By UFC Staff Report • Jul. 7, 2023

UFC International Fight Week 2023 comes to a thrilling close at T-Mobile Arena on July 8, as UFC 290 features a championship doubleheader guaranteed to shake the foundations of the MMA world.

Please enable Javascript to view this content

In the main event, the undisputed featherweight title is on the line when champion Alexander Volkanovski takes on interim titleholder Yair Rodriguez. Plus, it will be fireworks at 125 pounds when Brandon Moreno defends his flyweight crown against longtime rival Alexandre Pantoja.

How To Watch UFC 290 In Your Country

The UFC 290: Volkanovski vs Rodriguez Early Prelims kickoff Saturday July 8 at 6pm ET / 3pm PT in the United States on ESPN 2, ESPN+ and UFC Fight Pass. The Prelims air at 8pm ET / 5pm PT on ABC, ESPN and ESPN+ and the Main Card will air exclusively on ESPN+ in the U.S. at 10pm ET / 7pm PT.

Main event and co-main event scheduled for five rounds. All other bouts scheduled for three rounds.

MAIN CARD

Main Event - Featherweight Title Bout: Alexander Volkanovski (144.5) vs Yair Rodriguez (145)

Co-Main Event - Flyweight Title Bout: Brandon Moreno (125) vs Alexandre Pantoja (125)

Middleweight Bout: Robert Whittaker (185.5) vs Dricus Du Plessis (186)

UFC BREAKDOWN: Volkanovski vs Rodriguez | Moreno vs Pantoja 2

Lightweight Bout: Jalin Turner (158)* vs Dan Hooker (155.5)

Middleweight Bout: Bo Nickal (186) vs Val Woodburn (185.5)

PRELIMS

Welterweight Bout: Jack Della Maddalena (171) vs Josiah Harrell (170.5)
